# Excel Business Dashboards

This repository contains two professional Excel dashboards created for business data analysis, customer performance tracking, and sales insights.

---

## 📊 1. Customer Success Dashboard

**Features:**
- Customer retention & churn analysis  
- KPI cards for performance tracking  
- Segmentation & customer behaviour insights  
- Data cleaning and automation  
- Interactive charts and slicers  

---

## 📈 2. Sales Industry Data Dashboard

**Features:**
- Monthly & yearly sales trends  
- Region-wise performance  
- Top & bottom selling products  
- KPI visuals  
- Automated calculations using SUMIFS, Pivot Tables & Slicers
